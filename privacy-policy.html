Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

This policy describes how Kevin Carpenter collects and uses information for a limited, consent-based SMS notification testing program associated with the Leak Repair Board application. Leak Repair Board is the name of the application being tested, not a separate legal entity.

Information collected

Information may include a participant's name, mobile phone number, consent status and date, SMS opt-out requests, and technical message-delivery information. Test messages may include work-order-style identifiers and simulated assignment details.

How information is used

Information is used to obtain and document consent, send requested test notifications, evaluate message delivery and timing, handle opt-out and help requests, and maintain the security and operation of the test program.

Sharing

Mobile numbers and message information may be processed by telecommunications and SMS service providers solely to deliver and support the messages. We do not sell or share SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Consent and choices

Participation is voluntary. Participants are asked for express consent before being added to the test messaging list. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for assistance. Participants may also request removal by contacting the program operator.

Data retention

Participant information is retained only as reasonably needed to conduct testing, document consent, address support requests, and meet applicable legal obligations.
